**Time:** 3 minutes | **Where:** Control Plane | **User:** Alice

1. Open the **ConsoleUrl** in your browser.
2. Sign in as `alice@example.com` with the **WorkshopPassword**.
3. Take a moment to look at the left navigation:

   | Section | What you find there |
   |---|---|
   | **Build** | Your workflows, called *builds* in the console. Choose **+** to create one. |
   | **Library** | Shared building blocks: Tools, Identity, Memory, Evals, Policies and Guardrails. |
   | **Activity** | A log of sign-ins, changes, deploys and run actions. |
   | **AWS accounts** | Other accounts you can deploy to. You do not need this today. |

Workflows and runs are private to the user who created them until they are shared.

:::alert{type="info" header="Tip"}
Keep a private (incognito) browser window ready for Bob. You use it in Lab 1.
:::
